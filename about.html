About the Company

PT Haka Engineering Indonesia — a general trading and industrial engineering company based in Karawang, Jawa Barat.

Company Overview

Industrial Engineering & Technical Support

We focus on supporting industrial operations with coordinated engineering, maintenance, fabrication, electrical, and technical services. Our work is grounded in practical understanding of industrial environments and client requirements.

The company operates from Karawang, Jawa Barat, serving industrial needs across surrounding regions.
